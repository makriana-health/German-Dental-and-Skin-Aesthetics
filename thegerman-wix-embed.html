About THE GERMAN

German clinical standards, Bali calm.

THE GERMAN combines German medical standards with the calm natural beauty of Bali — precise dentistry and modern facial aesthetics in a structured, patient-focused environment. Founded by Dr. Michael Trolle (20+ years in dental care and clinic management in Germany, specializing in implantology and prosthetic dentistry) and Nina Trolle, M.Sc. (healthcare administration and clinical process optimization), the clinic is run by a multidisciplinary team of dental specialists, an orthodontist, and a dermatologist working from one integrated treatment plan.
